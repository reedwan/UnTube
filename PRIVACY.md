# UnTube Privacy Policy

UnTube does not collect, store, or transmit any user data.

All audio processing is performed locally on your computer in real time using WebAssembly. No audio or metadata is recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted over the Internet.

No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

UnTube is open source. The code is available at https://github.com/reedwan/UnTube.
